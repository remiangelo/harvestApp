# Harvest Help Center & FAQ

## Getting Started

### What is Harvest?

Harvest is a mindful dating app focused on creating real, meaningful connections. We use advanced algorithms to match you with compatible partners based on your interests, values, and personality.

### How do I create an account?

1. Download the Harvest app
2. Tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete the onboarding process

### Is Harvest free to use?

Yes! Harvest offers a free tier with core features. Premium subscriptions unlock additional features like unlimited likes, advanced filters, and priority matching.

## Profile & Onboarding

### How do I complete my profile?

During onboarding, you'll be asked to:

- Add your age and basic information
- Write a bio about yourself
- Upload photos (minimum 3 recommended)
- Select your interests and hobbies
- Set your preferences for matching
- Choose your relationship goals

### Can I edit my profile after onboarding?

Yes! Go to your Profile tab and tap the edit icon to update:

- Photos
- Bio
- Interests
- Preferences
- Location settings

### Why can't I upload certain photos?

Photos must:

- Show your face clearly
- Be appropriate (no nudity or offensive content)
- Be recent (within the last 2 years)
- Be of you (no group photos as main photo)

## Matching & Swiping

### How does matching work?

- Swipe right or tap ❤️ to like someone
- Swipe left or tap ❌ to pass
- Swipe up for a Super Like (limited daily)
- When both users like each other, it's a match!

### What's the difference between Like and Super Like?

- **Like**: Shows interest in someone
- **Super Like**: Shows extra interest and notifies the other person immediately (3 free per day)

### Can I undo a swipe?

Premium members can undo their last swipe using the rewind feature. Free users cannot undo swipes.

### Why am I not getting matches?

Tips to improve your matches:

- Add more photos (5-6 recommended)
- Write a detailed, authentic bio
- Be active daily
- Adjust your preferences if too restrictive
- Consider upgrading to Premium for more visibility

## Messaging & Communication

### When can I message someone?

You can only message users you've matched with. Once matched, either person can start the conversation.

### Are messages encrypted?

Yes, all messages are encrypted in transit. We take your privacy seriously.

### Can I video call through the app?

Video calling is available for matches who have been chatting for at least 24 hours. This helps ensure safety and genuine connections.

### Someone is harassing me. What should I do?

1. Tap the menu (three dots) in the chat
2. Select "Report User"
3. Choose the reason for reporting
4. Block the user if necessary
   We review all reports within 24 hours.

## Safety & Privacy

### How does Harvest protect my safety?

- **AI Red Flag Detection**: Automatically scans for suspicious behavior
- **Photo Verification**: Optional blue checkmark verification
- **Ready to Move Off App**: Safety check before sharing contact info
- **Report & Block**: Easy reporting of inappropriate behavior
- **24/7 Moderation**: Human review of reported content

### Is my data private?

- We never sell your data
- Location is approximate, not exact
- You control what information is visible
- GDPR and CCPA compliant
- End-to-end encryption for messages

### What is the "Ready to Move Off App" feature?

Before sharing contact information, our AI analyzes your conversation for safety concerns and provides recommendations. You must chat for at least 24 hours before this feature is available.

## Technical Issues

### The app is crashing. What should I do?

1. Update to the latest version
2. Restart your phone
3. Clear app cache (Android) or reinstall (iOS)
4. Contact support if issues persist

### My matches aren't loading

- Check your internet connection
- Pull down to refresh
- Log out and log back in
- Update the app

### I'm not receiving notifications

**iOS**: Settings > Notifications > Harvest > Allow Notifications
**Android**: Settings > Apps > Harvest > Notifications > Enable

### How do I delete my account?

1. Go to Settings
2. Scroll to "Account"
3. Tap "Delete Account"
4. Confirm deletion
   Note: This permanently deletes all your data and matches.
